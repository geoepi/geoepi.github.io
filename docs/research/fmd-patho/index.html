Abstract

This GeoEpi project brings together studies incorporating foot-and-mouth disease genomics, within-host dynamics, or animal experiment results. Registered work includes an agent-based disease transmission model, sequence mapping, incubation-phase transmission, and FMDV co-infection and recombination.
